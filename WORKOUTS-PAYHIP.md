## 01 · Full body A (gym)

**Etichete:** `Get Strong` · `Shape Up` · `Gym`

**Video:** `01-full-body-a-gym.mp4`

```
Warm-up
• Jump rope · 2 min
• World's greatest stretch · 5 / side

Workout
1. Goblet squat · 3 × 8–12
2. Lat pulldown · 3 × 8–12
3. Romanian deadlift · 3 × 8–12
4. Seated shoulder press · 3 × 8–12
5. Dead bug · 3 × 8 / side

Cool-down
• Child's pose reach · 30 s

Rest 60–90 s between sets. Write every set in your training log.
Beginner (weeks 1–2): 2 sets, light weight, stop with 4–5 reps in the tank.
Intermediate: as written, stop with about 2 reps in the tank. Hit the top of the range on every set? Add 1–2 kg next time.
Advanced: add 1 set and stop 1–2 reps before failure.
20 minutes only? Do 2 sets of each move and skip the last one.

General exercise, not medical advice. Stop if anything feels sharp.
```
