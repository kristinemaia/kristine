About

Hi, I'm Kristine.

KM

I help people who sit all day move better, recover faster and eat well, without making it complicated.

I've streamed on Twitch for five years and built a community of 72,000 people around the world. I studied sport and recovery: I hold a degree in Physical Education and Sport and I'm a certified recovery massage therapist, trained in recovery and sports massage and lymphatic drainage.

On stream I train, cook, paint and play games. Over the years my chat kept telling me the same thing: stiff necks, sore wrists, tight hips, too much takeaway. So I started doing something about it live, two minutes at a time. That became Move Monday, Cook with me and the hourly stretch break on game night, and it's what everything on this site is built on.

What I am

  • Sports science graduate (degree in Physical Education and Sport)
  • Certified recovery massage therapist (recovery & sports massage, lymphatic drainage)
  • Mobility & recovery coach

I'm not a medical professional, and nothing here is medical advice, a diagnosis or a treatment.

Jimmy, my dog, lives with my parents and makes the occasional guest appearance.
