About

Hi, I'm Kristine.

I help people who sit all day move better, recover faster and eat well, without making it complicated.

I've streamed on Twitch for five years and built a community of 72,000 people around the world. I studied sport: I hold a degree in Physical Education and Sport, and I coach mobility and recovery for people who sit all day.

On stream I train, cook, paint and play games. Over the years my chat kept telling me the same thing: stiff necks, sore wrists, tight hips, too much takeaway. So I started doing something about it live, two minutes at a time. That became the hourly stretch break when I game, and it's what everything on this site is built on.

What I am

  • Degree in Physical Education and Sport
  • Mobility & recovery coach
