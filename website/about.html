About

Hi, I'm Kristine

Your photo here
assets/kristine-about.jpg

I help people who sit all day move better, recover faster and eat well, without making it complicated.

I've streamed on Twitch for five years and built a community of 72,000 people around the world. Before and alongside that, I studied sport and recovery. I hold a degree in Physical Education and Sport and I'm a certified recovery massage therapist, trained in recovery and sports massage and lymphatic drainage. I also work with clients one-on-one in Bucharest.

On stream I train, cook, paint and play games. Over the years my chat kept telling me the same thing: stiff necks, sore wrists, tight hips, too much takeaway. So I started doing something about it live, two minutes at a time. That became Move Monday, Cook with me and the hourly stretch break, and it's what everything on this site is built on.

What I am and what I'm not

  • Sports science graduate (Physical Education and Sport)
  • Certified recovery massage therapist
  • Mobility and recovery coach
  • I'm not a doctor or a physiotherapist, and nothing here is medical advice.

Jimmy, my dog, lives with my parents and makes the occasional guest appearance.
