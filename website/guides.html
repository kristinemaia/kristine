Legs · squat

3 squat mistakes I see every leg day

If you sit all day, your hips and ankles get stiff, and the squat is where it shows. Here are the three mistakes I see most, and the one cue that fixes each.

What to fix

  1. Knees cave in. Push your knees out so they follow your toes, all the way down and all the way up.
  2. Chest drops. Keep your chest up and your eyes forward. Holding a dumbbell at your chest (goblet squat) makes this easier.
  3. Heels lift. Keep your whole foot down and your weight over the middle of your foot. If your heels still lift, squat to a bench and work on your ankles.

Try this

Film one set from the side with a light weight. Check one mistake at a time.

Who it is for

Beginners and anyone who sits for long hours. Skip it if your knees or hips hurt (not just feel stiff) and see a physio first.

Back · seated cable row

3 cable row mistakes I see in every gym

The seated row is one of the best moves against the desk hunch, if you do it right. These three mistakes move the work away from your upper back.

What to fix

  1. Shoulders rounded. Sit tall and set your shoulders back and down before every pull.
  2. Leaning back. Keep your torso still. If you have to swing to move the weight, it is too heavy.
  3. Elbows out. Keep your elbows close to your sides and pull the handle to your belly button.

Try this

Use a light weight for the first set and pause for one second with the handle at your belly.

Who it is for

Anyone who sits a lot and wants a stronger upper back. If you have shoulder or back pain, check with a doctor or physio first.

Shoulders · hang

Shoulders stiff from the mouse? Hang for 20 seconds

Hanging from a bar lets your shoulders and upper back get long after hours with your arms in front of you. It also trains your grip.

What to fix

  1. Grip. Hands about shoulder width, a full grip around the bar.
  2. Feet off, arms long. Let your body hang and breathe slowly. Start with 10 seconds and build up to 20.
  3. Can't hold it yet?. Keep your toes on the step or a box and take some of your weight. Step down, don't drop.

Try this

Two or three hangs of 10 to 20 seconds at the end of a session, or between sets.

Who it is for

Most people who sit all day. Skip it if you have a shoulder injury, shoulder pain or recent surgery, and ask your doctor first.
