Questions

Before you buy

Is this medical treatment?

No. I studied Physical Education and Sport and coach mobility. Everything here is general exercise and education, not a diagnosis or treatment. If you have pain or an injury, see a doctor.

How does 1:1 coaching work?

You tell me your goal, your schedule, your equipment and how you eat now. I build your training plan and your calorie and protein targets, you train and send me short videos of your sets, and every week we check in on your training and your habits and I adjust both. It is coaching, not treatment.

Do you help with food?

In 1:1 coaching, yes: calorie and protein targets, hand portions and food ideas that fit your week, for healthy adults. I'm not a dietitian, so I don't plan diets for medical conditions, pregnancy or eating disorders.

Do I need equipment?

A chair, a wall and a bit of floor space. Some routines use a resistance band, which is optional.

How do I get my product after paying?

You get an email with your download or access link right after payment.
