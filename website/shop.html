Questions

Before you buy

Is this medical treatment?

No. I have a degree in Physical Education and Sport and coach mobility. Everything here is general exercise and education, not a diagnosis or treatment. If you have pain or an injury, see a doctor.

How does online coaching work?

You tell me your goal, your schedule and what equipment you have. I build your plan, you train and send me short videos of your sets, and every week I check your form and adjust the plan. It is exercise coaching, not treatment.

Can you write me a meal plan?

No. I'm not a dietitian, so I don't write personal meal plans. I share general food ideas, like the quick recipes in my e-book.

Do I need equipment?

A chair, a wall and a bit of floor space. Some routines use a resistance band, which is optional.

How do I get my product after paying?

You get an email with your download or access link right after payment.
