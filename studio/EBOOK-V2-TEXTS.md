## Page 5 — replace the whole page (header and text)

**Header:** MEASURING FOOD WITH YOUR HANDS

**Text:**
> You don't need a scale to eat well. Your hands are always with you, they scale with your body size, and they're accurate enough for everyday meals. That's why I use them for every plate, at home, at a restaurant or at my desk between streams.
>
> Portion control matters because the same healthy food, eaten in the wrong amount, still adds up. Hand portions keep meals balanced without counting anything, and they work for any way of eating: vegetarian, pescetarian, omnivore, low-carb.
>
> **Protein:** one palm (the size and thickness of your palm, no fingers) ≈ 100–120 g of fish, tofu, chicken or eggs.
> **Carbs:** one cupped hand ≈ one serving of rice, pasta, oats, potatoes or bread.
> **Vegetables:** one fist ≈ one serving of greens, broccoli, carrots, peppers.
> **Fats:** one thumb ≈ one serving of oil, butter, nuts, cheese or avocado.
>
> A simple plate for most people: one palm of protein, one fist of vegetables, one cupped hand of carbs, one thumb of fat. Hungrier day or training day: add a second palm or a second cupped hand. Lighter day: drop the carbs to half a hand. The chart on the next page shows which foods go in each hand.

## Page 15 — replace the whole page (header and text)

**Header:** INTERMITTENT FASTING (fix the spelling: "INTERMITTENT")

**Text:**
> Intermittent fasting means you eat within a window and don't eat outside it. It isn't a diet; it's a schedule. I like it on stream days because it removes decisions: I know when I eat and when I don't.
>
> **Why people use it:** fewer eating decisions, easier calorie control, and for many people steadier energy. Research also points to effects on insulin sensitivity and cell repair, but the honest summary is that most of the benefit comes from eating less without feeling deprived.
>
> **The three common ways:**
> • **16/8:** fast 16 hours, eat within 8 (for example 12:00–20:00). The easiest to keep.
> • **5:2:** eat normally five days a week, keep two non-consecutive days very light (500–600 kcal).
> • **Alternate days:** one normal day, one very light day. The hardest to sustain; I don't recommend it as a start.
>
> **What's fine during the fast:** water, sparkling water, plain tea, black coffee, herbal tea. **What breaks it:** anything with calories, including milk in coffee, juice, sweetened drinks and most "zero" drinks with flavourings that make you hungry.
>
> **How to make it work:** start with 12 hours and add an hour every few days; keep busy in the fasting hours (a stretch break helps more than a snack); drink more water than you think; if you feel dizzy, shaky or can't focus, eat and shorten the window; tell a friend or your community what you're doing, it's easier with company.
>
> *Not medical advice. Don't fast if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, have or had an eating disorder, have diabetes or take medication that depends on meals, unless your doctor agrees.*
