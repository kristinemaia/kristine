The 5-minute desk stretch

Four spots. One chair. No equipment.

5:00Start the timer

Neck, shoulders, wrists and hips: the places a long day of sitting hits first. Start a 5-minute timer, move slowly and keep every stretch gentle. A comfortable pull is right. Sharp pain is a stop sign.

1

Neck

1:20
  • Chin tucks8 slow reps

    Sit tall and glide your chin straight back, like a gentle double chin. Hold 2 seconds.

  • Side neck stretch20 s each side

    Ear toward shoulder. Hold the chair seat with the other hand to keep that shoulder down.

2

Shoulders

1:00
  • Shoulder rolls8 rolls

    Lift, roll back and let the shoulders melt down. Big, slow circles, backwards only.

  • Shoulder blade squeeze8 reps

    Pull your shoulder blades back and down, hold 3 seconds, release.

3

Wrists

1:10
  • Palm-up stretch15 s each hand

    Arm straight, palm up. Gently pull the fingers back with your other hand.

  • Palm-down stretch15 s each hand

    Arm straight, palm down. Gently press the back of the hand toward you.

  • Tendon glides3 rounds

    Open hand, hook fist, full fist, flat fist, straight fingers. Slowly, one shape at a time.

4

Hips & back

1:30
  • Seated figure-430 s each side

    Ankle on the opposite knee. Sit tall and hinge forward a little until you feel the hip.

  • Seated twist15 s each side

    Hold the chair back and turn gently, starting from your upper back.

Page 2 · The details

Do each move well

Slow and gentle beats big and fast.

9moves explained

Page 1 is the routine. This page shows how to do each move well.

Neck

1:20
Chin tucks8 slow reps

HowSit tall. Slide your chin straight back, as if making a double chin. Keep your head level. Hold 2 seconds, then release.

FeelA light stretch at the back of your neck.

GentlerFewer reps and a smaller glide.

Side neck stretch20 s each side

HowHold the seat with your right hand. Let your left ear drift toward your left shoulder. Then switch sides.

FeelA stretch along the side of the neck that is holding the chair.

GentlerTilt less. Let the weight of your head do the work, no pulling.

Shoulders

1:00
Shoulder rolls8 rolls

HowLift your shoulders toward your ears, roll them back, then let them melt down. Big, slow circles, backwards only.

FeelYour shoulders loosening up with each circle.

GentlerSmaller circles.

Shoulder blade squeeze8 reps

HowPull your shoulder blades back and down, as if tucking them into your back pockets. Hold 3 seconds. Keep your neck long.

FeelThe work between your shoulder blades, not in your neck.

GentlerHold for 1 to 2 seconds.

Wrists

1:10
Palm-up stretch15 s each hand

HowArm straight, palm up. With your other hand, gently pull the fingers back toward the floor.

FeelA stretch along the inside of your forearm. No pain, no tingling.

GentlerBend the elbow a little and pull less.

Palm-down stretch15 s each hand

HowArm straight, palm down. Gently press the back of the hand toward you, fingers pointing down.

FeelA stretch along the top of your forearm.

GentlerBend the elbow and press less.

Tendon glides3 rounds

HowStraight hand, hook fist, full fist, flat fist, straight again. One shape at a time, like playing the keyboard in slow motion.

FeelSmooth, easy movement. Never forced.

GentlerOne hand at a time, slower.

Hips & back

1:30
Seated figure-430 s each side

HowCross one ankle over the opposite knee and flex your foot. Sit tall, then hinge forward from the hips until you feel it.

FeelA stretch in the outer hip and glute.

GentlerStay upright. A small lean is enough.

Seated twist15 s each side

HowHold the chair back with one hand and your opposite knee with the other. Turn gently, starting from your upper back.

FeelA mild turn through your upper and mid back. Keep breathing.

GentlerTurn less and keep your breathing easy.

Three rules for every move

Breathe slowly.Let the breath out as you go a little deeper.

Stop before it hurts.A comfortable pull is right. Sharp pain is a stop sign.

Both sides, same effort.Keep your shoulders relaxed and your jaw soft.
